Know the real health of your EV battery

BEV Diag reads your electric car's traction battery through an ELM327/STN OBD adapter and shows state of health, capacity, cell voltages and more. Starting with the Nissan Leaf.

Get support Android test builds

What it does

Battery health

State of charge, state of health, capacity in Ah and Hx, read straight from the battery controller.

Cell voltages

See every cell and spot a weak one before it costs you range.

Road test

A guided 20 to 80 km/h acceleration test with charts of power and voltage sag.

Session recording

Record drives with GPS and export them to CSV or JSON. Your data stays on your phone.

Bluetooth and WiFi

Works with common ELM327 and STN OBD adapters over BLE, Bluetooth or WiFi.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics. Read the policy.
