Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

The BEV Diag app does not collect your personal data. It has no account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking, and everything it reads stays on your device unless you choose to share it. This website has one optional sign-up form for news and beta invitations; what it collects is described below.

The app: data it handles

  • Vehicle and battery data (for example VIN, state of charge, cell voltages), read from your car through the OBD adapter. It is shown in the app and stored locally in recorded sessions.
  • Location, only while you record a session, to save the route, altitude and speed with it. It is stored locally.
  • Bluetooth and local network access, only to talk to your OBD adapter.

The app: where it goes

Nothing is uploaded to us. Recorded sessions live in the app's storage on your phone. If you export a session or send a problem report, the file goes only where you send it, for example your email app or the share sheet.

The test (beta) Android builds check a small public file on GitHub (latest.json) to learn whether a newer version exists. That request reveals your IP address to GitHub, as any web request does, and carries no data from the app.

Sign-up form on this website

Signing up is optional and based on your consent. You can withdraw it at any time.

What we collect: the e-mail address you enter, which phone you use (iPhone, Android or both), your car's make and model, and, only if you fill them in, the year, generation, battery capacity, whether the battery was replaced or modified (with your description), your OBD adapter and a free-text comment. We also store the time you gave consent and which version of the consent text you saw. We do not store your IP address.

Why: to tell you about BEV Diag news and to invite you to test the app, matching invitations to cars and phones. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell or share it for other purposes.

Where it is stored: in a database run by Amazon Web Services in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). It acts as our processor.

How long: until you ask us to delete it. Signing up again with the same e-mail address updates your earlier entry instead of creating a new one.

Your rights: you can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your data, or to stop contacting you, by writing to bevdiag@gmail.com. You can also complain to your national data protection authority.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services. The only third party involved in the sign-up form is Amazon Web Services, as described above.

Retention and deletion

You can delete any recorded session in the app, and uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will appear on this page.
